# Prototype scope

The public prototype provides general information from lawyer-approved knowledge-base material. It does not provide legal advice, assess a person's legal position, predict outcomes, create a lawyer-client relationship, or guarantee that a law or procedure is current.

If the approved knowledge base does not contain enough information, the assistant must say that it lacks enough information and refer the user to Alpha Advocates LLP.

# Substantive legal materials

Before the assistant is used to answer substantive Ethiopian-law questions, Alpha Advocates LLP should add lawyer-reviewed primary legal texts or summaries. Each source should identify the proclamation, regulation, directive, decision, or official publication; its number and date; its effective status; the date of the firm's review; and a stable source link where available.

Drafts, superseded laws, and unverified summaries should not be represented as current law. Time-sensitive questions, deadlines, calculations, and fact-specific questions should be referred to a lawyer.

# Privacy and confidentiality

Users should not provide names of opposing parties, identification numbers, financial-account details, health information, passwords, private documents, privileged communications, or detailed confidential case facts in the public assistant. The assistant is an information tool and is not an approved channel for transmitting confidential instructions to the firm.

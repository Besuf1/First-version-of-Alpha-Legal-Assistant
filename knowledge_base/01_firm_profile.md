# Firm overview

Alpha Advocates LLP is based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. The firm's journey began in November 2014 as Behailu Tesfahun and Associate Law Office. Alpha Advocates LLP was founded in 2025 as a continuation and development of that practice.

The firm describes itself as a strategic legal partner serving startups, investors, NGOs, creatives, companies, and changemakers. It combines legal knowledge with business and sector insight.

# Vision, mission, and values

The firm's vision is to support private-sector growth through client-centered, technology-enabled, and legally sound solutions. Its mission is to become the Alpha Standard in Ethiopian legal service delivery.

Its stated values are integrity, innovation, responsiveness, professionalism, client-centeredness, and teamwork.

# How the firm works

The firm emphasizes sector-specific knowledge in technology, media, investment, and real estate; proactive legal risk management; international-investor readiness; startup-friendly pricing options and flat-fee services; and service in Amharic and English.

# English — Current core sources

The core federal sources covered here are Federal Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2016, as amended by Income Tax Amendment Proclamation No. 1395/2025, Federal Tax Administration Proclamation No. 983/2016, and Value Added Tax Proclamation No. 1341/2024. Regulations and Ministry of Finance or Ministry of Revenue directives must also be checked.

The official Ministry of Justice webpage for the amendment displays an inconsistent year in part of its title, but the official gazette PDF identifies it as Proclamation No. 1395/2025, Federal Negarit Gazette No. 64, dated 1 September 2025. Use the gazette PDF and have counsel confirm citations.

# English — Business income tax

Income Tax Proclamation Article 18 imposes business income tax on taxable business income. Article 19(1) states a 30% business-income-tax rate for a body. Amendment Proclamation No. 1395/2025 changed individual business-income brackets and added special treatment for limited liability partnerships and collective investment funds, but did not replace the body rate in Article 19(1).

Taxable business income is not the same as gross sales. The Proclamation generally determines it from business income after allowable deductions, subject to detailed statutory rules, regulations, accounting standards, non-deductible expenses, depreciation, related-party, withholding, loss, and anti-avoidance provisions.

The assistant may state the general 30% body rate only with the source and amendment caveat. It must not calculate taxable income, deductions, loss carry-forwards, or tax due.

# አማርኛ — የንግድ ሥራ ገቢ ግብር

የገቢ ግብር አዋጁ አንቀጽ 18 ታክስ በሚከፈልበት የንግድ ሥራ ገቢ ላይ ግብር ይጥላል። አንቀጽ 19(1) ለድርጅት 30% የንግድ ሥራ ገቢ ግብር መጣኔ ይወስናል። የማሻሻያ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1395/2025 የግለሰቦችን የግብር ደረጃዎች ቀይሯል እና ለኃላፊነቱ የተወሰነ ሽርክናና ለጋራ የኢንቨስትመንት ፈንድ ልዩ ደንብ ጨምሯል።

ታክስ የሚከፈልበት የንግድ ገቢ ከጠቅላላ ሽያጭ ጋር አንድ አይደለም። ተፈቅደው የሚቀነሱ ወጪዎች፣ የሂሳብ ደረጃዎች፣ የእርጅና ቅናሽ፣ ኪሳራ፣ የተዛማጅ ወገኖች ግብይትና ሌሎች ዝርዝር ሕጎች ተፈጻሚ ይሆናሉ። ረዳቱ የሚከፈለውን ግብር ማስላት የለበትም።

# English — Value Added Tax (VAT)

VAT Proclamation No. 1341/2024 applies VAT to taxable supplies made by registered persons, taxable imports, and specified reverse-charged supplies. The rate is 0% for listed zero-rated supplies and 15% in other taxable cases. See Article 8. Exempt and zero-rated supplies are different and are listed in schedules to the Proclamation. See Articles 9–11 and the schedules.

Article 12 sets the compulsory registration threshold at ETB 2,000,000 of taxable supplies over the relevant twelve-month tests, or another amount specified by Ministry directive. The person generally must apply within seven days after becoming liable. Article 13 provides voluntary registration rules and contains its own threshold and conditions.

Because the Proclamation expressly allows the Ministry to change thresholds by directive, the assistant must present ETB 2,000,000 as the amount written in Article 12—not as a guaranteed current operational threshold—unless the latest directive is also checked.

# አማርኛ — ተጨማሪ እሴት ታክስ (VAT)

የVAT አዋጅ ቁጥር 1341/2024 በተመዘገበ ሰው በሚደረግ ታክስ የሚከፈልበት አቅርቦት፣ ታክስ በሚከፈልበት ገቢ ዕቃ እና በተወሰነ በገዢው የሚከፈል አቅርቦት ላይ VAT ይጥላል። በዜሮ መጣኔ ለተዘረዘሩ አቅርቦቶች 0%፣ በሌሎች ታክስ በሚከፈልባቸው አቅርቦቶች 15% መጣኔ ይተገበራል። አንቀጽ 8–11ንና የአዋጁን ሰንጠረዦች ይመልከቱ።

አንቀጽ 12 የግዴታ ምዝገባ መነሻን 2,000,000 ብር ወይም ሚኒስቴሩ በመመሪያ የሚወስነው ሌላ መጠን እንደሆነ ይገልጻል። ሕጉ መጠኑን በመመሪያ ለመቀየር ስለሚፈቅድ የወቅቱ መጠን በመመሪያ መረጋገጥ አለበት።

# English — Practical compliance checklist

A business should obtain tailored tax advice on:

- Taxpayer identification and taxpayer category.
- Accounting period, books, invoices, fiscal devices, and record retention.
- Business-income, employment, rental, withholding, dividend, interest, royalty, technical-service, and capital-gain rules.
- VAT compulsory or voluntary registration, tax invoices, input credits, returns, payment, exemptions, zero-rating, reverse charge, and digital/remote services.
- Turnover tax, excise, customs duties, pension, municipal, property, sector, and regional taxes where applicable.
- Related-party and transfer-pricing rules, foreign payments, permanent establishment, treaties, incentives, and tax clearance.
- Objections, appeals, audits, interest, penalties, and voluntary correction.

Do not send financial account numbers, passwords, full payrolls, tax credentials, or confidential ledgers to the public chatbot.

# አማርኛ — ተግባራዊ የግብር ዝርዝር

ድርጅት በሚከተሉት ጉዳዮች ልዩ የግብር ምክር ሊያገኝ ይገባል፦

- የግብር ከፋይ መለያና ደረጃ።
- የሂሳብ ዘመን፣ መዛግብት፣ ደረሰኞችና መዝገብ ማቆያ።
- የንግድ ገቢ፣ የቅጥር፣ የኪራይ፣ የተቀናሽ፣ የትርፍ ድርሻ፣ የወለድና የሮያሊቲ ግብር።
- የVAT ምዝገባ፣ የግብር ደረሰኝ፣ የግብአት ታክስ ቅናሽ፣ ሪተርን፣ ክፍያና ከሩቅ አገልግሎቶች።
- ተርንኦቨር ታክስ፣ ኤክሳይዝ፣ ጉምሩክ፣ ጡረታና ሌሎች ተገቢ ግብሮች።
- ኦዲት፣ ቅሬታ፣ ይግባኝ፣ ወለድና ቅጣት።

የባንክ ሂሳብ ቁጥር፣ የይለፍ ቃል፣ ሙሉ የደመወዝ መዝገብ፣ የግብር መግቢያ መረጃ ወይም ሚስጥራዊ የሂሳብ መዝገብ ለሕዝብ ቻትቦት አይላኩ።

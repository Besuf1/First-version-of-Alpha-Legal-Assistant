# English — Main legal framework

Commercial registration and licensing are primarily addressed by Commercial Registration and Licensing Proclamation No. 980/2016, as amended by Proclamation No. 1150/2019, together with Council of Ministers Regulation No. 392/2016 as amended by Regulation No. 461/2019. Sector-specific laws and regional or city-administration procedures can also apply.

The relevant authority depends on the business, investor, location, and sector. Specialized regulators issue licenses for activities such as mining, banking, insurance, microfinance, aviation, telecommunications, electricity, broadcasting, and other regulated activities. See Proclamation No. 980/2016, Article 21.

# አማርኛ — ዋና የሕግ ማዕቀፍ

የንግድ ምዝገባና ፈቃድ በዋናነት በንግድ ምዝገባና ፈቃድ አዋጅ ቁጥር 980/2016፣ በማሻሻያ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1150/2019፣ በሚኒስትሮች ምክር ቤት ደንብ ቁጥር 392/2016 እና በማሻሻያ ደንብ ቁጥር 461/2019 ይመራል። በዘርፍ የተለዩ ሕጎችና የክልል ወይም የከተማ አስተዳደር ሂደቶችም ሊተገበሩ ይችላሉ።

# English — Must registration happen before business starts?

An application for commercial registration must be submitted before commercial activity begins, using the prescribed form and attaching the documents required by regulations and directives. If accepted, the registering office registers the applicant and issues a registration certificate after the service fee is paid. A rejection must be communicated in writing with reasons. See Proclamation No. 980/2016, Article 6.

A business organization obtains legal personality upon registration. See Proclamation No. 980/2016, Article 7, and Commercial Code Article 175.

# አማርኛ — ንግድ ከመጀመሩ በፊት መመዝገብ ያስፈልጋል?

የንግድ ሥራ ከመጀመሩ በፊት የተዘጋጀውን ቅጽ በመሙላትና በደንብና በመመሪያ የተጠየቁ ሰነዶችን በማያያዝ የንግድ ምዝገባ ማመልከቻ መቅረብ አለበት። ማመልከቻው ከተቀበለ ክፍያ ከተፈጸመ በኋላ የምዝገባ ምስክር ወረቀት ይሰጣል። ውድቅ ከሆነ ምክንያቱ በጽሑፍ መገለጽ አለበት። የአዋጅ ቁጥር 980/2016 አንቀጽ 6ን ይመልከቱ።

የንግድ ማህበር በመመዝገቡ ሕጋዊ ሰውነት ያገኛል። የአዋጁን አንቀጽ 7 እና የንግድ ሕጉን አንቀጽ 175 ይመልከቱ።

# English — Is a business license also required?

Commercial registration and a business license are different steps. No person may engage in a business activity without a valid business license. The application must use the prescribed form and include documents required by the applicable regulation. See Proclamation No. 980/2016, Articles 22–24.

Some activities require a certificate of competence, professional license, sector approval, environmental approval, or another authorization before the business license can be issued. The chatbot should not promise that registration alone authorizes operation.

# አማርኛ — የንግድ ሥራ ፈቃድስ ያስፈልጋል?

የንግድ ምዝገባና የንግድ ሥራ ፈቃድ የተለያዩ ደረጃዎች ናቸው። የጸና የንግድ ሥራ ፈቃድ ሳይኖር በንግድ ሥራ መሰማራት አይቻልም። የአዋጅ ቁጥር 980/2016 አንቀጽ 22–24ን ይመልከቱ።

አንዳንድ ዘርፎች ፈቃድ ከመሰጠቱ በፊት የብቃት ማረጋገጫ፣ የሙያ ፈቃድ፣ የዘርፍ ተቆጣጣሪ ፈቃድ፣ የአካባቢ ፈቃድ ወይም ሌላ ፈቃድ ሊጠይቁ ይችላሉ።

# English — Renewal and changes

Business licenses must be renewed under Article 27. Proclamation No. 1150/2019 amended parts of the renewal and cancellation rules and supports registration and licensing through information technology and one-stop-shop services. Current renewal windows, fees, penalties, online procedures, and required documents must be checked with the competent authority because regulations, directives, and administrative practice may change.

Do not allow the assistant to calculate a late-renewal penalty or state that a cancelled license can be restored without reviewing the current law and facts.

Changes to a registered company's name, address, ownership, capital, management, business activity, memorandum, or other registered details may require authentication, registration, publication, tax updates, or sector approval. The exact steps depend on the change.

# አማርኛ — ፈቃድ ማደስና ለውጥ ማስመዝገብ

የንግድ ሥራ ፈቃድ በአንቀጽ 27 መሠረት መታደስ አለበት። የማሻሻያ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1150/2019 አንዳንድ የማደሻና የስረዛ ደንቦችን ቀይሯል። የወቅቱ የማደሻ ጊዜ፣ ክፍያ፣ ቅጣት፣ የመስመር ላይ ሂደትና ሰነዶች ከሚመለከተው ባለሥልጣን መረጋገጥ አለባቸው።

የድርጅት ስም፣ አድራሻ፣ ባለቤትነት፣ ካፒታል፣ አመራር፣ የንግድ ዓይነት ወይም መመስረቻ ጽሑፍ ሲለወጥ ማረጋገጥ፣ ማስመዝገብ፣ ማሳወቅ፣ የግብር መረጃ ማሻሻል ወይም የዘርፍ ፈቃድ ሊያስፈልግ ይችላል።

# English — General startup checklist

This is a planning checklist, not a complete legal requirement list:

1. Identify owners, nationality, funding source, and proposed activity.
2. Confirm whether the activity is open to the proposed owners and whether an investment permit is needed.
3. Choose the legal form with a lawyer and tax adviser.
4. Reserve or verify the company and trade names.
5. Prepare and authenticate the formation instrument where required.
6. Obtain a taxpayer identification number and complete commercial registration.
7. Obtain sector competence approvals and the business license.
8. Complete VAT, employment, pension, data-protection, IP, lease, customs, and local requirements as applicable.
9. Calendar renewals, returns, corporate approvals, and beneficial-ownership or change notifications.

# አማርኛ — አጠቃላይ የንግድ መጀመሪያ ዝርዝር

ይህ የዕቅድ ዝርዝር እንጂ ሙሉ የሕግ መስፈርት አይደለም፦

1. ባለቤቶችን፣ ዜግነትን፣ የገንዘብ ምንጭንና የታቀደውን ሥራ መለየት።
2. ሥራው ለባለቤቶቹ ክፍት መሆኑንና የኢንቨስትመንት ፈቃድ መፈለጉን ማረጋገጥ።
3. የድርጅት ቅርጽን ከጠበቃና ከግብር ባለሙያ ጋር መምረጥ።
4. የድርጅትና የንግድ ስምን ማረጋገጥ።
5. የመመስረቻ ሰነዶችን ማዘጋጀትና እንደአስፈላጊነቱ ማረጋገጥ።
6. የግብር ከፋይ መለያ ቁጥርና የንግድ ምዝገባ ማግኘት።
7. የዘርፍ ብቃት ማረጋገጫና የንግድ ፈቃድ ማግኘት።
8. እንደሚመለከተው የVAT፣ የሥራ፣ የጡረታ፣ የዳታ ጥበቃ፣ የአእምሯዊ ንብረት፣ የኪራይና የጉምሩክ መስፈርቶችን ማሟላት።

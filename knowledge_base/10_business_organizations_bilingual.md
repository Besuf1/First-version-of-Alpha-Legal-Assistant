# English — What business forms does the Commercial Code recognize?

Commercial Code Proclamation No. 1243/2021 lists these business organizations: general partnership, limited partnership, limited liability partnership, joint venture, share company, private limited company, and one-member private limited company. Except for a joint venture, a business organization acquires legal personality when it is registered in the commercial register. A joint venture is not disclosed to third parties. See Articles 172–175.

A business organization other than a joint venture must be established through a memorandum of association. The Code allows the relevant authority to prepare model memoranda but says the parties' freedom of contract must be respected. See Articles 173 and 177.

# አማርኛ — የንግድ ሕጉ የሚያውቃቸው የንግድ ማህበራት

የንግድ ሕግ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1243/2021 የሚከተሉትን የንግድ ማህበራት ይዘረዝራል፦ የአጠቃላይ ሽርክና፣ የተወሰነ ሽርክና፣ ኃላፊነቱ የተወሰነ ሽርክና፣ የጋራ ሽርክና (joint venture)፣ አክሲዮን ማህበር፣ ኃላፊነቱ የተወሰነ የግል ማህበር እና የአንድ አባል ኃላፊነቱ የተወሰነ የግል ማህበር። ከጋራ ሽርክና በስተቀር የንግድ ማህበር በንግድ መዝገብ ሲመዘገብ ሕጋዊ ሰውነት ያገኛል። አንቀጽ 172–175ን ይመልከቱ።

ከጋራ ሽርክና በስተቀር የንግድ ማህበር በመመስረቻ ጽሑፍ መቋቋም አለበት። አንቀጽ 173 እና 177ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — General partnership

In a general partnership, the partners are jointly and severally liable with the partnership for its obligations. An agreement attempting to limit this liability cannot be invoked against third parties. See Article 183.

**Plain-language meaning:** a creditor may be able to pursue a partner personally for a partnership obligation. A person considering this structure should obtain advice on personal exposure and the partnership agreement.

# አማርኛ — የአጠቃላይ ሽርክና

በአጠቃላይ ሽርክና ውስጥ አጋሮች ከሽርክናው ጋር በጋራና በተናጠል ለሽርክናው ግዴታዎች ኃላፊ ይሆናሉ። ይህን ኃላፊነት ለመገደብ የሚደረግ ስምምነት በሦስተኛ ወገኖች ላይ ሊቀርብ አይችልም። አንቀጽ 183ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — Limited liability partnership (LLP)

An LLP is formed by two or more persons to provide a licensed professional service and complementary services. Partners' liability is generally limited to their contributions, and the LLP has legal personality separate from its partners. See Articles 221–222.

The Code contains important exceptions. Where a partner or employee intentionally, fraudulently, or negligently causes damage in the partnership's work, the partnership may be jointly and severally liable without limit with that person. The LLP must have insurance for professional fault, with details to be set by the appropriate authority. See Article 228.

# አማርኛ — ኃላፊነቱ የተወሰነ ሽርክና (LLP)

ኃላፊነቱ የተወሰነ ሽርክና በሁለት ወይም ከዚያ በላይ ባለሙያዎች ፈቃድ የሚጠይቅ የሙያ አገልግሎትና ተጓዳኝ አገልግሎት ለመስጠት ይቋቋማል። በአጠቃላይ የአጋሮቹ ኃላፊነት ባደረጉት መዋጮ መጠን የተወሰነ ሲሆን ሽርክናው ከአጋሮቹ የተለየ ሕጋዊ ሰውነት አለው። አንቀጽ 221–222ን ይመልከቱ።

ሆኖም አጋር ወይም ሠራተኛ ሆን ብሎ፣ በማጭበርበር ወይም በቸልተኝነት ጉዳት ካደረሰ ልዩ የኃላፊነት ደንቦች ይተገበራሉ። አንቀጽ 228ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — Private limited company (PLC)

A PLC has capital that is fully paid in advance and divided into shares. Members are generally not liable for company debts after paying their contributions. Its shares cannot be offered for public subscription and it cannot issue transferable securities. A PLC must have at least two and no more than fifty members. See Article 495.

The Code states a minimum PLC capital of ETB 15,000 and a minimum par value of ETB 100 for each equal-value share. See Article 496. The PLC is formed when the capital is fully paid and the memorandum of association is entered in the commercial register. See Article 499.

# አማርኛ — ኃላፊነቱ የተወሰነ የግል ማህበር (PLC)

የPLC ካፒታል አስቀድሞ ሙሉ በሙሉ የተከፈለና በድርሻ የተከፈለ ነው። አባላት መዋጮአቸውን ሙሉ በሙሉ ከከፈሉ በኋላ በአጠቃላይ ለማህበሩ ዕዳ በግል ኃላፊ አይሆኑም። ድርሻው ለሕዝብ ምዝገባ ሊቀርብ አይችልም። PLC ከሁለት ያላነሱና ከሃምሳ ያልበለጡ አባላት ሊኖሩት ይገባል። አንቀጽ 495ን ይመልከቱ።

ሕጉ ዝቅተኛውን የPLC ካፒታል 15,000 ብር፣ የእያንዳንዱን ድርሻ የስም ዋጋ ቢያንስ 100 ብር እንዲሆን ይደነግጋል። አንቀጽ 496ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — One-member PLC

A one-member PLC may be incorporated by one person through a unilateral declaration. It has legal personality separate from its member, and the member is generally not personally liable once the contribution is fully paid. The minimum capital stated by the Code is ETB 15,000. See Articles 534–536.

A sole proprietor may convert an enterprise into a one-member PLC, but the trader remains jointly and severally liable with the company for debts incurred before the conversion. See Article 538.

# አማርኛ — የአንድ አባል PLC

አንድ ሰው በአንድ ወገን መግለጫ የአንድ አባል PLC ሊያቋቁም ይችላል። ማህበሩ ከአባሉ የተለየ ሕጋዊ ሰውነት አለው፤ አባሉም መዋጮውን ሙሉ በሙሉ ከከፈለ በኋላ በአጠቃላይ በግል ኃላፊ አይሆንም። ዝቅተኛው ካፒታል 15,000 ብር ነው። አንቀጽ 534–536ን ይመልከቱ።

ግለሰብ ነጋዴ ድርጅቱን ወደ የአንድ አባል PLC ሊለውጥ ይችላል፤ ነገር ግን ከለውጡ በፊት ለተፈጠሩ ዕዳዎች ከኩባንያው ጋር በጋራና በተናጠል ኃላፊ ሆኖ ይቀጥላል። አንቀጽ 538ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — Share company

A share company's capital is fixed in advance and divided into shares; its liabilities are met only by company assets, and shareholders' obligations are generally limited to their pledged contributions. See Article 245.

The Code states a minimum capital of ETB 50,000, a minimum ETB 100 par value per share, and at least five shareholders or founders, subject to other laws requiring otherwise. See Articles 247–248 and 252. Formation requirements include full subscription of capital and payment of at least one-quarter of the par value of cash shares into a blocked company account before registration. See Article 254.

# አማርኛ — አክሲዮን ማህበር

የአክሲዮን ማህበር ካፒታል አስቀድሞ የተወሰነና በአክሲዮኖች የተከፈለ ነው። የማህበሩ ግዴታዎች በማህበሩ ንብረት ይከፈላሉ፤ የባለአክሲዮኖቹም ግዴታ በገቡት መዋጮ የተወሰነ ነው። አንቀጽ 245ን ይመልከቱ።

ሕጉ ዝቅተኛውን ካፒታል 50,000 ብር፣ የእያንዳንዱን አክሲዮን የስም ዋጋ ቢያንስ 100 ብር እና ቢያንስ አምስት ባለአክሲዮኖች ወይም መስራቾች እንዲኖሩ ይደነግጋል። አንቀጽ 247–248 እና 252ን ይመልከቱ።

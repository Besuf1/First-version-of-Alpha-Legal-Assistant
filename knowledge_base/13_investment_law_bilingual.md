# English — Main framework

The main sources in this starter summary are Investment Proclamation No. 1180/2020, Investment Regulation No. 474/2020, and Investment Regulation No. 517/2022, published by the Ethiopian Investment Commission (EIC). Later Ethiopian Investment Board directives and sector laws may change who can invest, in what form, and under what conditions.

The Proclamation generally permits investment in any area unless contrary to law, morality, public health, or security. Areas reserved for joint investment with the government, domestic investors, or joint investment with domestic investors are specified by regulation. Other areas are generally open to foreign investment, but the Board may revise the list. See Article 6.

The assistant must not answer whether a particular activity is currently open to a foreign investor without checking the latest regulation, Board directive, and sector law.

# አማርኛ — ዋና የሕግ ማዕቀፍ

ይህ ማጠቃለያ በዋናነት በኢንቨስትመንት አዋጅ ቁጥር 1180/2020፣ በኢንቨስትመንት ደንብ ቁጥር 474/2020 እና በደንብ ቁጥር 517/2022 ላይ የተመሰረተ ነው። ቀጣይ የኢንቨስትመንት ቦርድ መመሪያዎችና የዘርፍ ሕጎች የተሳትፎ መስፈርቶችን ሊቀይሩ ይችላሉ።

አዋጁ በአጠቃላይ ከሕግ፣ ከሥነ-ምግባር፣ ከሕዝብ ጤና ወይም ከደህንነት ጋር እስካልተቃረነ ድረስ በማንኛውም የኢንቨስትመንት መስክ መሳተፍ ይፈቅዳል። ለመንግሥት፣ ለአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብት ወይም ለጋራ ኢንቨስትመንት የተከለሉ መስኮች በደንብ ይወሰናሉ። አንቀጽ 6ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — Forms of enterprise

Investment may be carried out through a sole proprietorship, an enterprise established in Ethiopia or abroad, a public enterprise, or a cooperative society. The enterprise must also be registered under the Commercial Code or other applicable law. See Article 8.

A foreign enterprise registered in Ethiopia is governed by the Ethiopian Commercial Code and other applicable enterprise laws. See Article 8(3).

# አማርኛ — የኢንቨስትመንት ድርጅት ቅርጾች

ኢንቨስትመንት በግለሰብ ንግድ፣ በኢትዮጵያ ወይም በውጭ በተቋቋመ ድርጅት፣ በመንግሥት የልማት ድርጅት ወይም በህብረት ሥራ ማህበር ሊከናወን ይችላል። ድርጅቱ በንግድ ሕጉ ወይም በሌላ ተገቢ ሕግ መሠረት መመዝገብ አለበት። አንቀጽ 8ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — Baseline minimum capital for foreign investors

Article 9 of Proclamation No. 1180/2020 states these baseline minimum capital amounts for one investment project:

- USD 200,000 for a foreign investor investing alone.
- USD 150,000 for a foreign investor investing jointly with a domestic investor.
- For architectural or engineering works, related technical consultancy, technical testing and analysis, or publishing: USD 100,000 when investing alone and USD 50,000 when investing jointly with a domestic investor.

Article 9 also contains exceptions, including certain reinvestment, board-member, and acquisition situations. Startup Proclamation No. 1396/2025, Article 40, creates a separate exception for qualifying foreign startups and foreign startup ecosystem builders that establish or invest in startups in Ethiopia.

These figures must never be given as a final answer without checking later laws and whether the person, sector, transaction, and project fall within an exception.

# አማርኛ — ለውጭ ባለሀብት መነሻ ዝቅተኛ ካፒታል

የአዋጅ ቁጥር 1180/2020 አንቀጽ 9 ለአንድ ፕሮጀክት የሚከተሉትን መነሻ መጠኖች ይጠቅሳል፦

- የውጭ ባለሀብት ብቻውን ሲያስተዳድር 200,000 የአሜሪካ ዶላር።
- ከአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብት ጋር በጋራ ሲያስተዳድር 150,000 የአሜሪካ ዶላር።
- በአርክቴክቸር፣ በኢንጂነሪንግ፣ በተዛማጅ ቴክኒክ ማማከር፣ በቴክኒክ ምርመራና ትንተና ወይም በማሳተም ሥራ ብቻውን 100,000 ዶላር፣ ከአገር ውስጥ ባለሀብት ጋር 50,000 ዶላር።

አንቀጽ 9 ልዩ ሁኔታዎችን ይዟል። በተጨማሪ የስታርታፕ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1396/2025 አንቀጽ 40 ለብቁ የውጭ ስታርታፖችና የስታርታፕ ምህዳር ገንቢዎች ልዩ ሁኔታ ይፈጥራል። ይህ መረጃ በወቅታዊ ሕግና በግለሰቡ ሁኔታ ሳይረጋገጥ የመጨረሻ መልስ ሊሆን አይችልም።

# English — Investment permits and incentives

Article 10 requires an investment permit for listed investors, including foreign investors, joint domestic-foreign investments, specified persons treated as domestic investors, and domestic investors seeking investment incentives in eligible areas.

Article 17 says eligible investment areas and the type and amount of incentives are determined by Council of Ministers regulation. An incentive is not automatic merely because a business is called an “investment.” Confirm the current regulation, location, sector, export conditions, permit, and approval.

# አማርኛ — የኢንቨስትመንት ፈቃድና ማበረታቻ

አንቀጽ 10 የውጭ ባለሀብቶችን፣ የአገር ውስጥና የውጭ የጋራ ኢንቨስትመንትን፣ በሕጉ መሠረት እንደ አገር ውስጥ ባለሀብት የሚቆጠሩ ሰዎችንና ማበረታቻ የሚፈልጉ አገር ውስጥ ባለሀብቶችን ጨምሮ ለተዘረዘሩ ባለሀብቶች የኢንቨስትመንት ፈቃድ ይጠይቃል።

የማበረታቻ ዓይነትና መጠን በደንብ ይወሰናል። አንቀጽ 17ን ይመልከቱ። አንድ ድርጅት “ኢንቨስትመንት” ተብሎ ስለተጠራ ብቻ ማበረታቻ አያገኝም።

# English — Protection and remittance

The Proclamation permits expropriation only for public interest, in accordance with law and on a non-discriminatory basis, with adequate advance compensation corresponding to prevailing market value. See Article 19.

Article 20 gives foreign investors a right, subject to applicable law, to remit specified payments and earnings in convertible foreign currency at the prevailing exchange rate. Listed items include profits and dividends; principal and interest on external loans; certain registered technology-transfer and collaboration payments; proceeds from transfers of shares or ownership; proceeds from sale, capital reduction, or liquidation; and expropriation compensation.

This is a legal guarantee, but actual documentation, tax clearance, banking, foreign-exchange, and registration requirements must be checked with the EIC, National Bank of Ethiopia, authorized bank, and counsel.

# አማርኛ — የኢንቨስትመንት ጥበቃና ገንዘብ ወደ ውጭ ማዛወር

አዋጁ ኢንቨስትመንት ለሕዝብ ጥቅም፣ በሕግ መሠረትና ያለ መድልዎ ብቻ እንዲወሰድ እና የወቅቱን የገበያ ዋጋ የሚመጥን በቂ ካሳ አስቀድሞ እንዲከፈል ይደነግጋል። አንቀጽ 19ን ይመልከቱ።

አንቀጽ 20 የውጭ ባለሀብት በተገቢው ሕግ መሠረት ትርፍና የትርፍ ድርሻ፣ የውጭ ብድር ዋና ገንዘብና ወለድ፣ በተመዘገበ የቴክኖሎጂ ሽግግር ስምምነት የሚከፈል ገንዘብ፣ ከድርሻ ሽያጭ ወይም ከድርጅት ማጣራት የሚገኝ ገንዘብና ካሳ ወደ ውጭ እንዲያዛውር ይፈቅዳል። የባንክ፣ የግብርና የሰነድ መስፈርቶች በተለይ መረጋገጥ አለባቸው።

# English — Foreign investor planning checklist

Before committing funds, confirm:

1. Investor classification and beneficial ownership.
2. Whether the activity is open, reserved, joint-venture-only, or sector-regulated.
3. Minimum capital and any applicable exception.
4. Investment-permit, company-registration, business-license, land/lease, construction, environmental, customs, tax, and sector approvals.
5. How foreign capital will be registered and evidenced.
6. Financing, external-loan registration, foreign-exchange, dividend, royalty, management-fee, and technology-transfer rules.
7. Tax incentives and their conditions, duration, location, and approval.
8. Employment and work permits for expatriate staff.
9. Exit, share transfer, liquidation, remittance, dispute resolution, and treaty protection.

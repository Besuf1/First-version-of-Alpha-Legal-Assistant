# English — Main framework

Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024 governs personal-data processing within its scope and establishes rights, duties, safeguards, regulatory powers, and penalties. A business should not assume the law applies only to technology companies; collecting customer, employee, applicant, vendor, visitor, website, app, CCTV, payment, or marketing data may involve personal-data processing.

# አማርኛ — ዋና ማዕቀፍ

የግል ዳታ ጥበቃ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1321/2024 በተፈጻሚነት ወሰኑ ውስጥ የግል ዳታ አሰባሰብና አቀናበርን ይመራል፣ መብቶችን፣ ግዴታዎችን፣ የደህንነት ጥበቃዎችንና ቅጣቶችን ይወስናል። ሕጉ ለቴክኖሎጂ ኩባንያዎች ብቻ አይደለም፤ የደንበኛ፣ የሠራተኛ፣ የአመልካች፣ የጎብኚ፣ የድረ-ገጽ፣ የአፕ፣ የCCTV ወይም የግብይት መረጃ መሰብሰብ ሊያስመለክተው ይችላል።

# English — Processing principles

Article 6 requires personal data to be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently; collected for explicit, specified, and lawful purposes; adequate, relevant, and not excessive; accurate and updated when necessary; kept no longer than necessary; protected for integrity, confidentiality, and security; and processed consistently with data sovereignty.

These principles support practical rules: collect only what is needed, explain why, keep it accurate, set retention periods, restrict access, and delete or anonymize it when no longer required.

# አማርኛ — የዳታ አቀናበር መርሆዎች

አንቀጽ 6 የግል ዳታ በሕጋዊ፣ ፍትሃዊና ግልጽ መንገድ እንዲቀናበር፣ ለተገለጸና ሕጋዊ ዓላማ ብቻ እንዲሰበሰብ፣ ከአስፈላጊው በላይ እንዳይሆን፣ ትክክለኛና ወቅታዊ እንዲሆን፣ ከሚያስፈልገው ጊዜ በላይ እንዳይቆይ፣ ሚስጥራዊነቱና ደህንነቱ እንዲጠበቅ ይጠይቃል።

# English — A lawful basis is required

Consent is one lawful basis, but it is not the only one. Article 7 also recognizes processing necessary for a contract or pre-contract steps requested by the data subject, compliance with legal obligations, protection of vital interests, specified public functions, and legitimate interests that are not overridden by the data subject's fundamental rights and freedoms. Sensitive personal data has additional requirements under Article 9.

A business should document the lawful basis for each processing purpose. Do not ask for consent where another legal basis is being relied upon, and do not treat silence or a bundled, forced choice as automatically valid consent. Article 8 contains consent conditions and withdrawal rules.

# አማርኛ — ሕጋዊ መሠረት ያስፈልጋል

ፈቃድ አንዱ ሕጋዊ መሠረት ነው፣ ግን ብቸኛው አይደለም። አንቀጽ 7 ለውል አፈጻጸም፣ ለሕጋዊ ግዴታ፣ ወሳኝ ጥቅምን ለመጠበቅ፣ ለተወሰነ የሕዝብ ተግባር ወይም የዳታ ባለቤቱን መሠረታዊ መብቶች በማይበልጥ ሕጋዊ ጥቅም ላይ መመስረትን ያውቃል። ጥንቃቄ የሚፈልግ የግል ዳታ ተጨማሪ መስፈርት አለው። አንቀጽ 9ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — Notice and data-subject rights

Article 24 requires a data controller to provide information such as its identity and contact details, purposes, lawful basis, recipients, cross-border transfers, retention period, available rights, withdrawal of consent, complaint rights, automated decision-making, and data categories. Timing differs depending on whether data is collected directly from the person or another source.

The Proclamation also contains rights of access, correction, deletion in applicable circumstances, restriction, objection, portability, and protections related to automated decisions. Each request requires identity, scope, exception, and deadline analysis; the assistant should not decide a specific request.

# አማርኛ — ማሳወቂያና የዳታ ባለቤት መብቶች

አንቀጽ 24 ዳታ ተቆጣጣሪው ስሙንና አድራሻውን፣ የማቀናበሩን ዓላማና ሕጋዊ መሠረት፣ የዳታ ተቀባዮችን፣ ወደ ውጭ የሚደረግ ማስተላለፍን፣ የማቆያ ጊዜን፣ ያሉ መብቶችን፣ ፈቃድን የማንሳትና ቅሬታ የማቅረብ መብትን እና ሌሎች መረጃዎችን እንዲገልጽ ይጠይቃል።

አዋጁ መረጃ የማግኘት፣ የማስተካከል፣ በተገቢው ሁኔታ የማጥፋት፣ የመገደብ፣ የመቃወምና ሌሎች መብቶችን ይዟል። የግለሰብ ጥያቄ ልዩ ግምገማ ይፈልጋል።

# English — Security, processors, and contracts

Article 16 requires care in choosing processors and generally requires processing by a processor to be governed by a written contract with instructions and equivalent confidentiality and integrity obligations. Article 17 requires appropriate technical and organizational measures against unauthorized or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage, with security proportionate to risk and the nature of data.

A vendor contract should address instructions, confidentiality, security, sub-processors, assistance with rights, incident reporting, return or deletion, audits, and cross-border transfers. This is an operational checklist, not a substitute for the full statutory requirements.

# አማርኛ — ደህንነት፣ ዳታ አቀናባሪና ውል

አንቀጽ 16 ዳታ አቀናባሪን በጥንቃቄ መምረጥና በአጠቃላይ ሥራው በጽሑፍ ውል፣ በመመሪያ፣ በሚስጥራዊነትና በደህንነት ግዴታ እንዲመራ ይጠይቃል። አንቀጽ 17 ያልተፈቀደ አቀናበር፣ የዳታ መጥፋት፣ መውደም ወይም መጎዳትን ለመከላከል ከአደጋው ጋር የሚመጣጠን የቴክኒክና የድርጅት እርምጃ ይጠይቃል።

# English — Breach notification

Article 43 generally requires the controller to notify the Authority within seventy-two hours after becoming aware of a personal-data breach. A processor must notify the controller without undue delay. The notification must contain the information listed in Article 43, and delay requires reasons.

Article 44 addresses communication to the affected data subject within seventy-two hours and provides exceptions. Whether notice is required and when the clock started are fact-specific legal questions; immediately activate the incident-response team and obtain counsel rather than relying on the chatbot.

# አማርኛ — የዳታ ጥሰት ማሳወቅ

አንቀጽ 43 ዳታ ተቆጣጣሪው የግል ዳታ ጥሰትን ካወቀ በአጠቃላይ በ72 ሰዓት ውስጥ ለባለሥልጣኑ እንዲያሳውቅ ይጠይቃል። ዳታ አቀናባሪውም ሳይዘገይ ለዳታ ተቆጣጣሪው ማሳወቅ አለበት።

አንቀጽ 44 ለዳታ ባለቤቱ በ72 ሰዓት ውስጥ ማሳወቅና ልዩ ሁኔታዎችን ይዟል። ጥሰት ሲከሰት በቻትቦት ላይ ከመመርኮዝ ይልቅ የአደጋ ምላሽ ቡድንንና ጠበቃን ወዲያውኑ ማነጋገር ያስፈልጋል።

# English — Small-business privacy checklist

1. Inventory personal data, purposes, systems, vendors, recipients, locations, and retention periods.
2. Record a lawful basis for every purpose and special basis for sensitive data.
3. Publish clear notices and create procedures for data-subject requests.
4. Minimize fields and access; secure accounts, devices, backups, and transfers.
5. Sign compliant processor agreements and control sub-processors.
6. Review cross-border transfers, data sovereignty, direct marketing, cookies, CCTV, biometrics, children, and automated decisions.
7. Perform impact assessments where required and appoint a data-protection officer where applicable.
8. Maintain an incident plan capable of meeting the seventy-two-hour rules.
9. Train staff and document decisions, testing, deletion, and review.

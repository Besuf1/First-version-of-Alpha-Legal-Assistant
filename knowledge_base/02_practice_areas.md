# Startup and technology law

Alpha Advocates LLP supports founders from incorporation through investment rounds. Its listed startup and technology services include founder agreements, SAFE and convertible-note matters, licensing, compliance, trademark and copyright registration, trade secrets, and licensing arrangements.

# Corporate and commercial law

The firm provides corporate and commercial support throughout the business lifecycle, including formation, contracts, governance, and restructuring.

# Mergers and acquisitions

The firm supports business acquisitions, equity transactions, and cross-border investments. Listed services include legal due diligence, transaction structuring, and shareholder agreements.

# Tax and customs advisory

The firm advises businesses on tax and customs compliance, planning, and audits.

# Construction and real estate

The firm supports developers, investors, and property owners. Listed services include land-lease matters, contract negotiation, permits, disputes, and joint-development arrangements.

# Litigation and dispute resolution

The firm provides courtroom advocacy in civil, commercial, labor, and administrative disputes.

# Employment and labor law

The firm assists with employment contracts, workplace policies, and employment-law advisory aligned with Ethiopian law.

# Media, advertising, and entertainment

The firm assists creatives, publishers, platforms, and advertisers. Listed work includes content licensing, intellectual-property clearance, and regulatory compliance.

# NGO and charity support

The firm helps local and international NGOs with registration, operations, governance, and compliance.

# International investment and trade

The firm supports foreign investors entering Ethiopia, including joint-venture and regulatory-navigation matters.

# Regulatory compliance and due diligence

The firm provides sector-specific regulatory and compliance guidance and document preparation. It also conducts legal due diligence for buyers, sellers, and investors to identify risk and assess legal readiness.

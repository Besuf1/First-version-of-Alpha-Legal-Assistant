# English — Are electronic records legally recognized?

Electronic Transaction Proclamation No. 1205/2020 says information must not be denied legal effect only because it is in an electronic message. A legal requirement for writing may be satisfied where the electronic information is available electronically, accessible, and capable of being retained for later reference. See Articles 7–8.

Where a law requires a signature but does not prescribe its type, an electronic method may satisfy the requirement if it identifies the person, indicates approval, and is appropriately reliable in the circumstances. The Proclamation provides reliability factors. See Article 9.

An electronic record may satisfy an original-form requirement if there is reliable assurance of integrity and it remains accessible and intelligible. Electronic messages are admissible as evidence, but their evidential weight depends on reliability, integrity, identification of the originator, and other relevant circumstances. See Articles 12–13.

**Important:** some transactions may have special authentication, registration, witnessing, notarization, or sector requirements. The assistant must not say that clicking “I agree” makes every Ethiopian contract enforceable.

# አማርኛ — ኤሌክትሮኒክ ሰነዶች በሕግ ይታወቃሉ?

የኤሌክትሮኒክ ትራንዛክሽን አዋጅ ቁጥር 1205/2020 መረጃ በኤሌክትሮኒክ መልዕክት መልክ ብቻ ስለቀረበ ሕጋዊ ውጤት እንዳይነፈገው ይደነግጋል። መረጃው በኤሌክትሮኒክ መልክ የሚገኝ፣ ተደራሽና ለወደፊት ማጣቀሻ ሊቀመጥ የሚችል ከሆነ የጽሑፍ መስፈርትን ሊያሟላ ይችላል። አንቀጽ 7–8ን ይመልከቱ።

ሕግ ፊርማ ሲጠይቅ የፊርማውን ዓይነት ካልወሰነ፣ ሰውየውን የሚለይ፣ ፈቃዱን የሚያሳይና እንደሁኔታው አስተማማኝ የሆነ ኤሌክትሮኒክ ዘዴ መስፈርቱን ሊያሟላ ይችላል። አንቀጽ 9ን ይመልከቱ።

ኤሌክትሮኒክ መልዕክቶች በማስረጃነት ሊቀርቡ ይችላሉ፤ የማስረጃ ክብደታቸው ግን በአስተማማኝነት፣ በይዘት ሙሉነትና በላኪው መለያ ላይ ይመሰረታል። አንቀጽ 12–13ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — Arbitration clauses

Arbitration and Conciliation Working Procedure Proclamation No. 1237/2021 requires an arbitration agreement to be in writing and recognizes an accessible electronic record as written. Article 6 contains the detailed form rules.

Not every dispute may be arbitrated. Article 7 excludes listed matters, including criminal and tax cases, bankruptcy judgments, dissolution decisions for business organizations, general land matters including lease, and other matters prohibited by law. The exact scope must be checked by a lawyer.

Unless law or agreement provides otherwise, arbitration proceedings and awards are confidential. See Article 39.

የግልግል ዳኝነትና የዕርቅ አሠራር ሥርዓት አዋጅ ቁጥር 1237/2021 የግልግል ስምምነት በጽሑፍ እንዲሆን ይጠይቃል፤ ለወደፊት ጥቅም ተደራሽ የሆነ ኤሌክትሮኒክ መረጃም የጽሑፍ መስፈርትን ሊያሟላ ይችላል። ዝርዝሩን በአንቀጽ 6 ይመልከቱ።

ሁሉም ጉዳዮች ወደ ግልግል ሊቀርቡ አይችሉም። አንቀጽ 7 የወንጀል፣ የግብር፣ የመክሰር ውሳኔ፣ የንግድ ማህበር መፍረስ ውሳኔና አጠቃላይ የመሬት ጉዳዮችን ጨምሮ የማይቀርቡ ጉዳዮችን ይዘረዝራል።

በሕግ ወይም በስምምነት ሌላ ካልተወሰነ የግልግል ሂደትና ውሳኔ ሚስጥራዊ ይሆናል። አንቀጽ 39ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — Commercial contract review checklist

This checklist is practical information, not a statutory formula and not a substitute for legal drafting:

- Correct legal names, registration numbers, addresses, and signing authority.
- Clear description of goods, services, specifications, milestones, acceptance, and change control.
- Price, currency, tax, invoice, payment date, withholding, interest, and bank details.
- Term, renewal, suspension, termination grounds, notice, and consequences of termination.
- Ownership and licensing of existing and newly created intellectual property.
- Confidentiality, permitted disclosures, data protection, cybersecurity, and records.
- Warranties, indemnities, liability limits, insurance, and force-majeure allocation.
- Compliance with licensing, employment, anti-corruption, tax, customs, sanctions, and sector rules.
- Governing law, dispute escalation, court or arbitration forum, seat, language, and service of notices.
- Entire-agreement, amendment, assignment, subcontracting, waiver, severability, and priority clauses.

The assistant may explain this list generally. It must not declare a contract safe, complete, binding, or suitable for a user's facts.

# አማርኛ — የንግድ ውል ምርመራ ዝርዝር

ይህ ተግባራዊ ዝርዝር እንጂ በሕግ የተወሰነ ቀመር ወይም የጠበቃ ሥራ ምትክ አይደለም፦

- የተዋዋዮች ትክክለኛ ሕጋዊ ስም፣ ምዝገባ ቁጥር፣ አድራሻና የፈራሚ ሥልጣን።
- ዕቃው ወይም አገልግሎቱ፣ ጥራት፣ የሥራ ደረጃ፣ ተቀባይነትና የለውጥ ሂደት።
- ዋጋ፣ ምንዛሬ፣ ግብር፣ ደረሰኝ፣ የክፍያ ጊዜና የግብር ቅናሽ።
- የውሉ ጊዜ፣ ዕድሳት፣ እገዳ፣ ማቋረጥ፣ ማስታወቂያና ውጤት።
- የአእምሯዊ ንብረት ባለቤትነትና ፈቃድ።
- ሚስጥራዊነት፣ የዳታ ጥበቃ፣ ሳይበር ደህንነትና መዝገብ አያያዝ።
- ዋስትና፣ ካሳ፣ የኃላፊነት ገደብ፣ ኢንሹራንስና ከአቅም በላይ ሁኔታ።
- የሚተገበር ሕግ፣ የክርክር መፍትሔ፣ ፍርድ ቤት ወይም ግልግል፣ ቋንቋና ማስታወቂያ።

# English — NDA checklist

A non-disclosure agreement should usually identify: the parties; the purpose; what counts as confidential information; exclusions; permitted use; who may receive the information; required safeguards; compelled disclosure; duration; return or destruction; IP ownership and no implied license; remedies and dispute resolution.

An NDA does not automatically protect every item forever, override a legal disclosure duty, transfer IP, or replace employment, data-protection, trade-secret, or cybersecurity controls. Fact-specific drafting and enforceability require lawyer review.

# አማርኛ — የሚስጥር ጥበቃ ስምምነት (NDA) ዝርዝር

NDA በአጠቃላይ ተዋዋዮችን፣ ዓላማን፣ ሚስጥራዊ መረጃ ምን እንደሆነ፣ ከጥበቃ ውጪ የሆኑ መረጃዎችን፣ የተፈቀደ አጠቃቀምን፣ መረጃውን ማን ሊያገኝ እንደሚችል፣ የደህንነት ግዴታዎችን፣ በሕግ የሚጠየቅ ገለጻን፣ የጥበቃ ጊዜን፣ መመለስ ወይም ማጥፋትን፣ የአእምሯዊ ንብረትንና የክርክር መፍትሔን ሊገልጽ ይገባል።

NDA ሁሉንም መረጃ ለዘላለም አይጠብቅም፣ በሕግ የሚጠየቅ ገለጻን አያግድም፣ የአእምሯዊ ንብረትን በራሱ አያስተላልፍም።

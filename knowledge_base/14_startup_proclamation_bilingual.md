# English — Status and purpose

Startup Proclamation No. 1396/2025 was published in Federal Negarit Gazette No. 65 on 2 September 2025 and entered into force on publication. It creates a federal framework for startup and startup-ecosystem-builder designation, support, financing, incentives, regulatory sandboxes, and related institutions. See Articles 1, 3, and 44.

Detailed procedures depend on regulations and directives. The availability of a legal benefit in the Proclamation does not prove that the application portal, implementing directive, budget, fund, or administrative service is operational for a particular applicant.

# አማርኛ — የአዋጁ ሁኔታና ዓላማ

የስታርታፕ አዋጅ ቁጥር 1396/2025 በፌዴራል ነጋሪት ጋዜጣ ቁጥር 65 መስከረም 2 ቀን 2025 ታትሞ በዚያው ቀን ተፈጻሚ ሆኗል። አዋጁ ለስታርታፕና ለስታርታፕ ምህዳር ገንቢዎች ስያሜ፣ ድጋፍ፣ ፋይናንስ፣ ማበረታቻና የቁጥጥር ሳንድቦክስ ማዕቀፍ ይፈጥራል። አንቀጽ 1፣ 3 እና 44ን ይመልከቱ።

ዝርዝር ሂደቶች በደንብና በመመሪያ ይወሰናሉ። በአዋጁ ውስጥ ጥቅም መጠቀሱ ፖርታሉ፣ ፈንዱ ወይም አገልግሎቱ ለአመልካቹ በተግባር ተዘጋጅቷል ማለት አይደለም።

# English — What is a startup?

Article 2 defines a startup as a person or group with no or limited business history engaged in creating economic value through introducing, creating, adapting, transferring, or reverse-engineering an innovative, technology-based or technology-enabled product, service, or process that is scalable and market-changing.

Designation is not mandatory simply to operate as a startup or ecosystem builder. However, designation is a prerequisite for the incentives and privileges under the Proclamation. See Article 6.

# አማርኛ — ስታርታፕ ምንድን ነው?

አንቀጽ 2 ስታርታፕን ምንም ወይም የተወሰነ የንግድ ታሪክ ያለው፣ በኢኖቬሽን፣ በቴክኖሎጂ ወይም በቴክኖሎጂ በታገዘ፣ ሊሰፋ የሚችልና ገበያን ሊለውጥ የሚችል ምርት፣ አገልግሎት ወይም ሂደት ኢኮኖሚያዊ ዕሴት የሚፈጥር ሰው ወይም ቡድን በማለት ይገልጻል።

ስታርታፕ ተብሎ ሥራ ለመሥራት ስያሜ መውሰድ በራሱ ግዴታ አይደለም። ነገር ግን በአዋጁ የተዘረዘሩ ማበረታቻዎችንና ጥቅሞችን ለማግኘት ስያሜ ቅድመ ሁኔታ ነው። አንቀጽ 6ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — Eligibility and application

A person seeking startup designation must satisfy the statutory startup definition. Article 7 further provides that an unregistered individual may be designated when the stated requirements are met. The applicant must own, or demonstrate ownership of, the product, process, or service; founders must hold at least 25% of capital; and the applicant cannot be a public company.

An organization with a valid license and established for no more than five years may apply for designation. See Article 7(3). This is an initial-application rule; it is different from the maximum total period for which designation may remain valid.

Applications are to be submitted through the Digital Startup Platform using the required form and attachments. The designation committee may request an interview, demonstration, or inspection. Article 9 states that the application should receive a response within thirty working days. Detailed documents and procedures are to be set by directive.

# አማርኛ — ብቁነትና ማመልከቻ

ስያሜ የሚፈልግ አመልካች በአዋጁ የተገለጸውን የስታርታፕ ትርጉም ማሟላት አለበት። አመልካቹ የሚያቀርበው ምርት፣ አገልግሎት ወይም ሂደት ባለቤት መሆን ወይም ባለቤትነቱን ማሳየት፣ መስራቹ ቢያንስ 25% ካፒታል መያዝና አመልካቹ የሕዝብ ኩባንያ አለመሆን አለበት። አንቀጽ 7ን ይመልከቱ።

የጸና ፈቃድ ያለውና ከተቋቋመ ከአምስት ዓመት ያልበለጠ ድርጅት ለስያሜ ማመልከት ይችላል። ማመልከቻው በዲጂታል ስታርታፕ ፕላትፎርም በኩል በቅጽና በአባሪ ሰነዶች ይቀርባል። ዝርዝሩ በመመሪያ ይወሰናል። አንቀጽ 9ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — Duration and ongoing duties

A startup designation is valid for two years. Renewal should be requested thirty working days before expiry. The total validity of designation cannot exceed eight years. See Article 13.

A designated startup must comply with applicable law, pursue its growth objectives, provide non-personal information requested by the Ministry, report on incentives and results, notify specified structural or business changes within ten working days, use incentives properly, and maintain proper books. See Article 12.

Designation may be suspended or revoked for statutory reasons. A suspended startup is not eligible for incentives during suspension. See Articles 14–15.

# አማርኛ — የስያሜ ጊዜና ቀጣይ ግዴታዎች

የስታርታፕ ስያሜ ለሁለት ዓመት ይጸናል። የዕድሳት ማመልከቻ ስያሜው ከማብቃቱ 30 የሥራ ቀናት በፊት መቅረብ አለበት። አጠቃላይ የስያሜ ጊዜ ከስምንት ዓመት ሊበልጥ አይችልም። አንቀጽ 13ን ይመልከቱ።

ስያሜ ያገኘ ስታርታፕ ሕጎችን ማክበር፣ የዕድገት ግቦቹን መከተል፣ ለሚኒስቴሩ መረጃና ሪፖርት ማቅረብ፣ የድርጅት ወይም የንግድ ለውጥን በ10 የሥራ ቀናት ውስጥ ማሳወቅ፣ ማበረታቻዎችን በአግባቡ መጠቀምና የሂሳብ መዝገብ መያዝ አለበት። አንቀጽ 12ን ይመልከቱ።

# English — Incentives

The Proclamation creates a startup grant program, a national credit-guarantee framework, and a fund-of-funds structure. Eligibility, funding, operation, and practical availability depend on implementing measures and budget. See Articles 23–29 and 35–36.

Under Article 30, a designated startup may benefit from applicable investment incentives and, during designation, from listed income-tax, dividend-tax, and withholding-tax exemptions in accordance with a Ministry of Finance directive. Applications for tax exemption must be verified by the Ministry and submitted to the Ministry of Finance for approval. Non-convertible grants, incentives, gifts, and donations are addressed in Article 30(3). Article 31 provides loss-carry-forward rules.

Article 33 permits a designated startup to import necessary capital goods duty-free after submitting the list and obtaining Ministry of Finance approval. Article 34 addresses ecosystem builders.

The assistant must not promise an exemption, refund, grant, guarantee, or duty-free approval. It should explain the statutory framework and refer the applicant for current implementation checks.

# አማርኛ — ማበረታቻዎች

አዋጁ የስታርታፕ ግራንት ፕሮግራም፣ የብድር ዋስትና ማዕቀፍና የፈንዶች ፈንድ መዋቅር ይፈጥራል። በተግባር መገኘታቸው በደንብ፣ በመመሪያ፣ በበጀትና በአስተዳደራዊ አፈጻጸም ይወሰናል። አንቀጽ 23–29 እና 35–36ን ይመልከቱ።

አንቀጽ 30 ስያሜ ያገኘ ስታርታፕ በገንዘብ ሚኒስቴር መመሪያ መሠረት በስያሜው ጊዜ ከተወሰኑ የገቢ፣ የትርፍ ድርሻና የተቀናሽ ግብሮች ነፃ ሊሆን እንደሚችል ይደነግጋል። ማመልከቻው በሚኒስቴሩ ተረጋግጦ ለገንዘብ ሚኒስቴር መቅረብና መጽደቅ አለበት።

አንቀጽ 33 አስፈላጊ የካፒታል ዕቃዎችን ከቀረጥ ነፃ ለማስገባት ዝርዝር ለገንዘብ ሚኒስቴር ቀርቦ ፈቃድ እንዲገኝ ይጠይቃል። ረዳቱ ጥቅም እንደሚገኝ ዋስትና መስጠት የለበትም።

# English — Regulatory sandbox, foreign participation and work permits

The Ministry may establish a regulatory sandbox for testing innovative products, services, and processes, with eligibility and procedures to be set by directive. See Article 37.

Subject to applicable work-permit law, a foreign national joining a designated startup is entitled under Article 39 to a three-year work permit; Article 39 also refers to a startup visa. Procedures must be confirmed with the responsible authorities.

Article 40 says the minimum capital requirement in Investment Proclamation Article 9 does not apply to foreign startups and foreign startup ecosystem builders establishing or investing in startups in Ethiopia. They may access incentives under the Startup Proclamation, but foreign startups are excluded from the grant program. Registration and licensing details are to be set by Ethiopian Investment Board directive.

# አማርኛ — ሳንድቦክስ፣ የውጭ ተሳትፎና የሥራ ፈቃድ

ሚኒስቴሩ አዳዲስ ምርቶችን፣ አገልግሎቶችንና ሂደቶችን ለመፈተሽ የቁጥጥር ሳንድቦክስ ሊያቋቁም ይችላል። ዝርዝር ብቁነትና ሂደት በመመሪያ ይወሰናል። አንቀጽ 37ን ይመልከቱ።

ተገቢው የሥራ ፈቃድ ሕግ እንደተጠበቀ ሆኖ፣ አንቀጽ 39 ስያሜ ባለው ስታርታፕ የሚቀጠር የውጭ ዜጋ ለሦስት ዓመት የሚጸና የሥራ ፈቃድና የስታርታፕ ቪዛ እንዲያገኝ ይጠቅሳል። ተግባራዊ ሂደቱ ከሚመለከተው ባለሥልጣን መረጋገጥ አለበት።

አንቀጽ 40 ብቁ የውጭ ስታርታፖችና የውጭ ስታርታፕ ምህዳር ገንቢዎች በኢንቨስትመንት አዋጁ አንቀጽ 9 ያለው ዝቅተኛ ካፒታል እንደማይመለከታቸው ይደነግጋል። የውጭ ስታርታፖች ግን ከግራንት ፕሮግራሙ ተጠቃሚ አይሆኑም።
